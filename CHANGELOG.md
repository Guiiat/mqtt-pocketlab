## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-03

### Added

- Embedded MQTT 3.1/3.1.1 broker and local live web console.
- Topic, client, retained-state, QoS, message-stream, and throughput observability.
- Seeded climate, CO₂/air-quality, and electricity-meter simulators.
- Smart-home, greenhouse, and micro-factory scenarios.
- Disconnect, jitter, and abnormal-spike fault injection.
- UTF-8/binary NDJSON recording and timing-aware replay.
- CLI, REST/SSE API, bilingual documentation, integration tests, and cross-platform CI.
- Localhost-only default plus authenticated explicit remote mode and bounded inputs.
