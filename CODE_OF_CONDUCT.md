# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in MQTT PocketLab a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, accept responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what benefits the community. Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of others' private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the repository's security/contact facilities. Maintainers may remove content or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens a safe, constructive community.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
